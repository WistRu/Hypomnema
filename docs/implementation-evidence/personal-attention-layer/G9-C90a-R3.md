## Accepted scope

C90a-R3 closes the provider-ledger seam that R2b deliberately left open:

- the final C81 attempt atomically adopts its exact active handoff reservation
  immediately before provider binding, with no extra job event;
- ordinary C81 and adopted final work share the hard daily provider-attempt cap,
  while the C90 coordinator remains provider-free and retains its separate
  start-based policy;
- daily committed USD is actual attributed cost plus every active reservation
  remainder, plus one legacy implicit remainder only for a running job that has
  no active explicit reservation;
- a same-day explicit reservation contributes no second candidate increment;
- unbound work rolls its reservation to the current UTC date at bind time, while
  bound attempts retain immutable attribution to their bind/adoption date;
- positive provider usage requires a current matching adoption and raises actual
  usage and reservation consumption atomically;
- terminal job events close only the remaining reservation amount; retry and
  recovery events keep it active;
- public and raw-SQL bind/adoption paths fail closed on missing, inactive,
  cross-day or tampered reservation envelopes.

Schema 23/24 paths do not prepare schema-25 SQL and retain their legacy behavior.
The worker binds before `provider.start()`, so any rejected adoption/bind produces
zero provider calls.

## Independent correction loop

The first green candidate was not accepted. Read-only audits found and closed:

1. positive C90 progress usage;
2. legacy implicit remainder multiplication across historical retries;
3. adoption-time and bind-time cap races;
4. raw-SQL C90 provider binding;
5. direct reservation consumption and direct terminal release;
6. missing reservation-day and committed-cost insertion guards;
7. cross-day adoption/bind ambiguity;
8. prebind positive token/cost usage;
9. final-only rather than ordinary-plus-final daily attempt accounting;
10. paired reservation forgery that shifted `reserved` and `consumed` equally;
11. a negative test whose exact error depended on SQLite BEFORE-trigger order.

Every production finding received a rollback test. The final test-only correction
keeps the in-lease adoption mismatch exact and treats the out-of-lease case as a
deterministic rejection with a full row rollback assertion.
