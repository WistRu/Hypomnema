## Audit remediation

The first independent audit rejected the packet with two P1 and two P2 gaps.
The accepted tree closes all four:

- ledger publication now rejects provider/model/prompt/request, generated-time
  or cumulative-usage provenance that differs from the exact terminal attempt;
- redacted detail retains `claimDigest`, `rationaleDigest` and `locatorDigest`;
- report query/path integers accept only canonical positive decimal strings
  within `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` (`041`, `1e2`, `5.0`, signs and whitespace
  are rejected);
- list batch-loads and fully validates provenance, terminal chain, claims,
  evidence, source/snapshot heads and payloads for every emitted report.

Additional executable regressions prove a real catalog corruption becomes only
`{error:"INTERNAL_ERROR"}` over HTTP, a schema-23 `createApp` does not register
research routes, an empty Resource/no-run is readable, and corruption in older
history cannot hide behind a valid newest item.
