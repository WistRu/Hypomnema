## Packet outcome

`W80a` makes the accepted captured-only research workflow reachable from the
Library without adding browser navigation or URL inference:

- page, resolved Resource and deduplicated bulk-selection entry points use the
  closed `ResearchTarget` contract and remain hidden when the research writer is
  disabled;
- the first-party preflight shows exact coverage arithmetic, approved budget,
  acquisition level, safe snapshot/source identities, revisions, digests,
  chunk byte ranges and truncation, limitations and normalized origins;
- authenticated and sensitive origins require fresh per-run confirmation;
  successful submission clears consent, while an unknown-outcome retry keeps
  the same approval and idempotency key;
- missing, failed and recoverable `empty_captured_text` sources can enter a
  user-confirmed, sequential exact-instance capture batch. Mixed corpus can be
  improved before launch, while hard privacy/format exclusions remain
  non-capturable;
- durable job status and cancellation survive closing/remounting the panel and
  reconcile to a newer target run after a terminal job.

## Identity, Resource resolution and race proof

Bulk selection maps every selected physical tab with bounded concurrency before
deduplicating logical pages and applying the 100-page research limit. It neither
silently drops physical inputs nor caps them before logical identity is known.
Exact capture is limited to at most 100 already-open copies, runs sequentially,
records each success/failure and never opens, navigates or automatically retries
a page.

TabDrawer reads the server's closed ResourceResolution. Resolved pages enter the
exact Resource target. Unmatched/ambiguous pages expose an explicit
classification-review action; the Resources surface deterministically locates
and focuses the requested logical page even beyond the first 50 queue entries,
where the existing explicit candidate/existing-Resource override is available.

The panel is keyed by target identity. Approval, capture, consent and active-job
state cannot leak to a replacement target, and late preflight/submit responses
cannot clear or submit newer state. Loading and failed copy/scope/mapping queries
show actionable states rather than the false conclusion that no open copies
exist.

## Accessibility and localization
